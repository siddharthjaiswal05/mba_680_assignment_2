Investor survey

Six short sections. There are no right answers. Just pick what you would really do. Your answers stay on this device. At the end you can download them as a file and send it to whoever is collecting the group's data. If you want, the portal will also build you a portfolio straight away.

1

About you

Use your roll number, not your name, so the data set stays anonymous.

2

Sure money, or a coin flip?

Eight rows. In each one you can take a sure amount, or flip a coin for double or nothing. Pick whichever you would actually choose. Both sides are worth the same on average, so there is no trick.

3

Money now, or more money later?

Six rows. Each one offers Rs 10,000 today, or a bigger amount if you wait.

#Take nowOr wait and getWait how longYour pick
4

What you are like

Thirty short sentences. Say how much each one sounds like you. Go quickly, your first reaction is usually the right one.

5

Your utility curve

First pick the lowest and highest amounts that feel relevant to you. Then, for each middle amount, move the slider to the chance of winning the big prize that would make the two choices feel exactly equal to you.

6

Gains and losses

Same idea, but now about winning and losing rather than about how much you end up with. First tell us the amount you are measuring gains and losses against.

7
